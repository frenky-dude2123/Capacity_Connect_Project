Key Architecture Takeaways

In this module, we examine how cross-region active-active architectures distribute traffic load during peak demand periods, maintaining an SLA of 99.999% availability.

  • Anycast DNS Routing: Directs users to the geolocated edge PoP with the lowest latency ping.
  • Conflict Resolution (CRDTs): How concurrent writes across primary regions reconcile without distributed locking bottlenecks.
  • Circuit Breaker Cascades: Preventing brownouts by shedding non-essential load when CPU capacity utilization exceeds 85%.
